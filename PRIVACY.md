# JKBrowseSafe Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 2026

## Summary
JKBrowseSafe has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. We do not collect, sell or share your browsing data.

## What Stays Only On Your Device
- Browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs and the downloads list
- Site permissions (camera, microphone, location), per-site JavaScript choices and sites you blocked
- Settings such as search engine and theme
- Crash logs. Web addresses and email addresses are removed. Nothing is uploaded; you choose whether to share a log.
You can delete it in Settings (Clear browsing data, Site settings, JavaScript, Blocked sites). Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Private Tabs
Private tabs do not save history and do not use the normal cache.

## Network Connections The App Makes
- The websites you visit and the search engine you choose. Their own privacy policies apply.
- Once a day, the app downloads public protection lists:
  Disconnect tracking-protection list (from GitHub), URLhaus malware list (abuse.ch) and Phishing Army phishing list.
  These are plain downloads. Your history and the pages you visit are NOT sent.
- On Android, the built-in Android System WebView may use Google Safe Browsing to check whether a page is dangerous. This is run by Google under Google's privacy policy.

## Reporting Unsafe Sites
If you use "Report unsafe site", the reporting website opens in a new tab with the address you are reporting. Nothing is sent unless you submit that form.

## Permissions
Camera, microphone and location are used only when a website asks and you allow it. You can change this anytime in Site settings.

## Children
JKBrowseSafe is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes (for example, if ads are added), the new policy will be published here and shown in the app before the change takes effect.
